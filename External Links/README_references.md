# Reference Literature — External Links

All PDFs in this folder are source documents used in the Integrated Imaging and Genomics Report.
Cross-referenced against CT and ultrasound imaging findings and patient genomic data.

---

## Files in This Folder

### 1. Unilateral true hermaphrodite with 46,XX/46,XY dispermic chimerism — PMC
**File:** `Unilateral true hermaphrodite with 46,XX_46,XY dispermic chimerism - PMC.pdf`  
**Source:** PMC1050410  
**URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1050410/  
**Relevance:** Documents clinical anatomy of XX/XY dispermic chimera including rudimentary uterus, right fallopian tube and ovary, and functioning testis on contralateral side. Directly relevant to bilateral adnexal findings and Müllerian zone signal in imaging.

---

### 2. Magnetic Resonance Imaging Findings of Ovotesticular Disorder of Sex Development with Bilateral Gonadoblastoma
**File:** `Magnetic Resonance Imaging Findings of Ovotesticular Disorder of Sex Development with Bilateral Gonadoblastoma.pdf`  
**Source:** Iranian Journal of Radiology — brieflands.com/articles/iranjradiol-56259.html  
**URL:** https://brieflands.com/articles/iranjradiol-56259.html  
**Relevance:** MRI imaging findings in OT-DSD. Documents Müllerian remnants, gonadal position, and mixed gonadal tissue appearance on imaging. Cross-reference for posterior midline uterine zone findings.

---

### 3. Normal adrenal gland thickness on computerized tomography in an Asian Indian adult population — PMC
**File:** `Normal adrenal gland thickness on computerized tomography in an Asian Indian adult population - PMC.pdf`  
**Source:** PMC6319091  
**URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6319091/  
**Relevance:** Provides reference values for normal adrenal gland CT measurements: mean limb thickness 4.1–4.9 mm, mean HU ~32.66, volume R: 3.62 cm³, L: 4.84 cm³. Used as baseline for adrenal comparison in Finding 1.

---

### 4. Imaging Aspects in a Case of Persistent Müllerian Duct Syndrome (PMDS): A Case Report and Overview — PMC
**File:** `Imaging Aspects in a Case of Persistent Müllerian Duct Syndrome (PMDS)_ A Case Report and Overview - PMC.pdf`  
**Source:** PMC11364497  
**URL:** https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11364497/  
**Relevance:** Documents CT and MRI imaging of Müllerian duct remnants in a patient with male external genitalia. Directly relevant to the anterior midline abdominal structure finding (Finding 5) and posterior uterine zone (Finding 3).

---

### 5. 46,XX/46,XY Chimerism and Human Sexual Development — OBM Genetics
**File:** `obm.genet.2202156.pdf`  
**Source:** OBM Genetics — lidsen.com  
**URL:** https://www.lidsen.com/journals/genetics/genetics-06-02-156/obm.genet.2202156.pdf  
**Relevance:** Comprehensive review of XX/XY chimerism and phenotypic variability. Documents range of internal anatomy, Müllerian structure prevalence, gonadal composition, and developmental outcomes. Used as primary reference for chimerism-anatomy cross-reference throughout report.
